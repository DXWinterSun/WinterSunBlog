**🧨 Episode 07｜You Took My Door**

*“我本来以为我能出去的……直到你把门带走了。”*

📍 市政厅某个黄昏。

Phil实在忍不了了。Sam最近不仅不说话，还连续一周加班，早上7点就来，晚上9点才走，像是在故意和人错开一切交集。

他某天拍着Sam的办公桌，劈头盖脸就是一句：

“**Face it, dude!** She’s not into you anymore. Stop trying to become Mr. Perfect-for-the-boss. It’s over. Move on!”

Sam愣住了。他本来正在对一份预算申请书发呆，那一刻像是魂被拽回来，整个人都失去重心。

他缓缓转头，看着Phil，脸上没有愤怒，只有……一种委屈到极致的茫然。

“I’m not trying to be perfect for *Liz*.”

“Then why the hell are you acting like a tax-deductible robot??” 😤

Sam深吸一口气，站起来，一字一顿地说：

“Because I thought… maybe I was finally moving on.

I thought if I focused、worked hard、did everything right……

then one day I’d look up，and I’d be ready to open that door.”

“The door out of this.”

“Out of guilt. Out of ‘what ifs’. Out of Liz.”

“But then **you showed up with her**—” 他手指向Phil，“and you started laughing with her, and bringing her coffee, and watching movies, and knowing her favorite type of *quiet*. And I realized—”

他声音有些哑了，像是终于把压在心底的东西挖出来：

“**You didn’t just walk through my door.**

**You took it with you.**

Now I’m standing in a room with no way out.”

Phil：😵

“Dude. Dude. DUDE.” Phil双手抬起，像被泼了一桶冰水：“Are you freaking telling me you thought I was DATING her???”

Sam的沉默默认一切。

Phil狂摇头：“I have a girlfriend! Back in the US!! I talk to her every night! She knows about Winter! She *ships you two*, man!”

Sam：“…what.”

Phil：“And *Winter*? She’s been asking me about your favorite type of tea, your dumb poetry notebook, and your tiger tooth that she says is cute—”

Sam：“She said that???”

Phil：“Yes, dude, while giggling. Like, *giggling*! Have you ever seen her giggle at my face? NO! She thinks *you* are the cinnamon roll, not me!!”

💥现场爆炸气氛拉满！

**🤝 Episode 08｜You Idiots Like Each Other**

*“你爱她，她喜欢你，我被卷进来当你们爱情的教练了。”*

📍市政厅某个仓库间——Phil和Sam刚刚经历一场真相炸裂的对峙。

沉默了一会儿，Phil突然叹气，用手揉着脸说：

“This is *so* stupid.

I thought you still liked Liz,

and *you* thought I liked Winter,

and *she* thought you were still hung up on Liz,

and *I* was just trying to eat my lunch.”

Sam声音低低的：“Wait… she thought I still liked Liz?”

Phil翻白眼：“Dude. You avoided her like she had a restraining order on you, worked overtime every night, didn’t speak unless spoken to. What was she supposed to think?!”

Sam：“I thought she hated me.”

Phil：“She thought you were healing.”

Sam眨了眨眼，好像灵魂卡壳了一秒。

然后两人对视了一会儿，**同时发出一声带着叹息的笑——**

Phil：“We’re both idiots, huh?”

Sam：“Massive idiots.”

他们靠近了一步，Phil张开双臂：“Come on, bring it in.”

Sam也笑了一下，抱了上去。

**两个大男孩在一堆旧投影仪和打印纸中间抱了一下。**

一秒、两秒、三秒。

第四秒，Phil拍了拍Sam的背：“Okay. Now. Be honest.”

“Do you wanna go after her?”

Sam顿住了。他没有立刻回答。只是轻轻吸了口气，低声说：

“I’ve wanted to since the moment she said my name like it was worth something.”

Phil笑了笑，后退一步，拍拍他肩膀。

“Then you better hurry up, bro. Before she starts asking *me* what your favorite lunch order is.”

Sam立刻拔腿就走。

Phil在后面喊：“(It’s tuna and pickles, by the way!!)”

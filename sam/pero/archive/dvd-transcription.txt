BELLINI

THE GRANDDADDY OF ALL COLLINWOODISMS, AND THE DREAM
HEIST OF A LIFETIME.

FOR COLLINWOOD’S FORGOTTEN WORLD OF PETTY THIEVES,
IT CAN BE A HOPE GREATER THAN HEAVEN, LIKE THE BURIED

BELLINI CONTINUED

SCREENSHOT: E__WELCOME_TO_COLLINWOOD_20261107_184025.093.jpg

THE TERM COMES FROM NEIGHBORHOOD LEGEND
BOLLO BELLINI WHO, DESPITE A JUVENILE
CRIMINAL RECORD, GOT A JOB DOWNTOWN AT THE
FEDERAL RESERVE WHERE HE WAS PUT IN CHARGE OF
BURNING THE OLD MONEY.

AFTER TWENTY YEARS WITHOUT MISSING A SINGLE DAY OF
WORK, SUDDENLY, HE DISAPPEARED. A FAVORITE COLLINWOOD
PASTIME IS TO SPECULATE ABOUT THE UNTOLD FORTUNE
HE MUST HAVE TAKEN WITH HIM.

Definitions and Etymologies

MULLINSKI

SCREENSHOT: E__WELCOME_TO_COLLINWOOD_20261107_184030.413.jpg

SOMEONE WHO CLAIMS RESPONSIBILITY FOR THE
CRIME OF ANOTHER, IN EXCHANGE FOR PAYMENT, OF COURSE.
AFTER ELIOT NESS BROUGHT DOWN CAPONE AND CLEANED
UP CHICAGO, HE CAME TO CLEVELAND TO DO THE SAME.

MULLINSKI CONTINUED

SCREENSHOT: E__WELCOME_TO_COLLINWOOD_20261107_184036.092.jpg

JUST AS NESS WAS ABOUT TO NAIL HIS FIRST
MOB LUMINARY HERE, A FORMER TROLLY CAR
DRIVER NAMED JOE MULLINSKI TURNED HIMSELF IN,
CONFESSING TO THE CRIMES OF WHICH THE
MOBSTER STOOD ACCUSED.

AS NESS TARGETED THE NEXT MOBSTER, YET ANOTHER
MAN CONFESSED, AND SO ON, AND SO ON. NESS SOON
DEVELOPED A SERIOUS ALCOHOL PROBLEM AND DIED IN
CLEVELAND, A BITTER AND FAILED MAN.

Definitions and Etymologies

KRAZNER

SCREENSHOT: E__WELCOME_TO_COLLINWOOD_20261107_184040.646.jpg

A BRICK WALL THAT’S MORTARED WITH AN UNSTABLE
MIXTURE OF SAND AND WATER.

AFTER REMAINING STUMPED FOR OVER A DECADE BY A SERIES
OF BANK ROBBERIES IN WHICH THIEVES INEXPLICABLY BATTERED
HOLES THROUGH SEEMINGLY SOLID WALLS, A FRUSTRATED
CLEVELAND DETECTIVE PUNCHED ONE SUCH WALL,
BREAKING IT AND NOT HIS HAND.

KRAZNER CONTINUED

SCREENSHOT: E__WELCOME_TO_COLLINWOOD_20261107_184051.109.jpg

POLICE EVENTUALLY ARRESTED
MICKEY KRAZNER, A BRICKLAYER IN THE
EMPLOY OF COLLINWOOD CONSTRUCTION CO, WHICH HAD

THEREFORE, NOT BEING ITS CREATOR, DOESN’T
DESERVE THE EPONYM.)

Definitions and Etymologies

ANTWERP / KUPUCHNIK

SCREENSHOT: E__WELCOME_TO_COLLINWOOD_20261107_184056.233.jpg

ANTWERP

A TELESCOPING TOOL DESIGNED TO COLLAPSE A KRAZNER.
IN COLLABORATION WITH MICKEY KRAZNER (SEE ABOVE),
INFAMOUS SAFECRACKER AND TOOLSMITH JERZY ANTWERP
DEVELOPED THIS INDISPENSABLE COMPLIMENT TO KRAZNER’S WALL.

KUPUCHNIK

A TRAGEDY BROUGHT ABOUT BY A CURSE. NOT EVEN THE OLDEST
PERSON IN COLLINWOOD CAN REMEMBER THE ORIGIN OF THIS
TERM, WHICH MUST SURELY BE A MOST UNFORTUNATE STORY.

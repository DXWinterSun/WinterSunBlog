COLLINWOODISMS

Bellini

一生梦寐以求的那一票中文释义

↑ 目录

BELLINI · DVD 页面

英文原文 DVD SOURCE

THE GRANDDADDY OF ALL COLLINWOODISMS, AND THE DREAM HEIST OF A LIFETIME.

FOR COLLINWOOD’S FORGOTTEN WORLD OF PETTY THIEVES, IT CAN BE A HOPE GREATER THAN HEAVEN, LIKE THE BURIED TREASURE OF PIRATE LORE.

中文译文 整理翻译

所有 Collinwood 黑话中的老祖宗，也是人一生梦寐以求的那一票。

对 Collinwood 那群被遗忘的小偷而言，它可以是比天堂更大的盼头，如同海盗传说里埋藏的宝藏。

BELLINI CONTINUED · DVD 页面

英文原文 DVD SOURCE

THE TERM COMES FROM NEIGHBORHOOD LEGEND BOLLO BELLINI WHO, DESPITE A JUVENILE CRIMINAL RECORD, GOT A JOB DOWNTOWN AT THE FEDERAL RESERVE WHERE HE WAS PUT IN CHARGE OF BURNING THE OLD MONEY.

AFTER TWENTY YEARS WITHOUT MISSING A SINGLE DAY OF WORK, SUDDENLY, HE DISAPPEARED. A FAVORITE COLLINWOOD PASTIME IS TO SPECULATE ABOUT THE UNTOLD FORTUNE HE MUST HAVE TAKEN WITH HIM.

中文译文 整理翻译

这个词来自街区传说中的 Bollo Bellini。他虽然有过少年犯罪记录，却在市中心的联邦储备机构找到了一份工作，负责焚烧旧钞。

二十年里，他一天班也没缺过，之后却突然消失了。猜测他一定带走了多少难以想象的财富，成了 Collinwood 居民喜爱的消遣。

Mullinski

↑ 目录

MULLINSKI · DVD 页面

英文原文 DVD SOURCE

SOMEONE WHO CLAIMS RESPONSIBILITY FOR THE CRIME OF ANOTHER, IN EXCHANGE FOR PAYMENT, OF COURSE. AFTER ELIOT NESS BROUGHT DOWN CAPONE AND CLEANED UP CHICAGO, HE CAME TO CLEVELAND TO DO THE SAME.

中文译文 整理翻译

替别人的罪行承担责任的人，当然，是要收钱的。Eliot Ness 扳倒 Capone、整顿了芝加哥之后，又来到克利夫兰，打算如法炮制。

MULLINSKI CONTINUED · DVD 页面

英文原文 DVD SOURCE

JUST AS NESS WAS ABOUT TO NAIL HIS FIRST MOB LUMINARY HERE, A FORMER TROLLY CAR DRIVER NAMED JOE MULLINSKI TURNED HIMSELF IN, CONFESSING TO THE CRIMES OF WHICH THE MOBSTER STOOD ACCUSED.

AS NESS TARGETED THE NEXT MOBSTER, YET ANOTHER MAN CONFESSED, AND SO ON, AND SO ON. NESS SOON DEVELOPED A SERIOUS ALCOHOL PROBLEM AND DIED IN CLEVELAND, A BITTER AND FAILED MAN.

中文译文 整理翻译

正当 Ness 要拿下他在这里盯上的第一个黑帮大人物时，一位名叫 Joe Mullinski 的前有轨电车司机主动投案，承认了那名黑帮分子被指控的罪行。

当 Ness 把目标转向下一个黑帮分子时，又有另一个人出来认罪，如此这般，一次又一次。Ness 很快出现了严重的酗酒问题，最终满怀怨愤、失意地死在了克利夫兰。

原图拼作 TROLLY，按原样保留。以上涉及 Eliot Ness 的叙述仅为 DVD 中的词源故事，不作为已核实的历史记载。

Krazner

用不牢固灰浆砌成的砖墙中文释义

↑ 目录

KRAZNER · DVD 页面

英文原文 DVD SOURCE

A BRICK WALL THAT’S MORTARED WITH AN UNSTABLE MIXTURE OF SAND AND WATER.

AFTER REMAINING STUMPED FOR OVER A DECADE BY A SERIES OF BANK ROBBERIES IN WHICH THIEVES INEXPLICABLY BATTERED HOLES THROUGH SEEMINGLY SOLID WALLS, A FRUSTRATED CLEVELAND DETECTIVE PUNCHED ONE SUCH WALL, BREAKING IT AND NOT HIS HAND.

中文译文 整理翻译

用沙和水调成的不牢固混合物作灰浆砌成的砖墙。

十多年来，一连串银行抢劫案令警方百思不得其解：窃贼竟能在看似坚固的墙上砸出洞来。一名沮丧的克利夫兰警探朝这样一面墙挥了一拳，结果碎的是墙，不是他的手。

KRAZNER CONTINUED · DVD 页面

英文原文 DVD SOURCE

POLICE EVENTUALLY ARRESTED MICKEY KRAZNER, A BRICKLAYER IN THE EMPLOY OF COLLINWOOD CONSTRUCTION CO, WHICH HAD BUILT EACH OF THE ROBBED BANKS. (THE TERM IS RARE ON THE STREETS AS SOME CLAIM KRAZNER IMPORTED THE TECHNIQUE FROM HIS NATIVE CROATIA, AND, THEREFORE, NOT BEING ITS CREATOR, DOESN’T DESERVE THE EPONYM.)

中文译文 整理翻译

警方最终逮捕了 Mickey Krazner。他是 Collinwood 建筑公司的砌砖工，而所有遭劫的银行都由这家公司建造。（这个词在街头并不常用，因为有人说 Krazner 是从故乡克罗地亚带来了这项技术。因此，他并非发明者，不配让它以自己的名字命名。）

ANTWERP · DVD 页面

英文原文 DVD SOURCE

A TELESCOPING TOOL DESIGNED TO COLLAPSE A KRAZNER. IN COLLABORATION WITH MICKEY KRAZNER (SEE ABOVE), INFAMOUS SAFECRACKER AND TOOLSMITH JERZY ANTWERP DEVELOPED THIS INDISPENSABLE COMPLIMENT TO KRAZNER’S WALL.

一种专门用来弄塌 Krazner 墙的伸缩工具。臭名昭著的保险箱窃贼兼工具匠 Jerzy Antwerp 与 Mickey Krazner（见上文）合作，开发了这件与 Krazner 的墙配套、不可或缺的工具。

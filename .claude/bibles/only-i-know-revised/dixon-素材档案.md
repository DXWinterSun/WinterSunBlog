## 人物与称呼

- **Officer Jason Dixon**，Ebbing 警局警员，Willoughby 警长手下。全镇叫他 Dixon。
- 叫他 **Jason** 的：Willoughby（当面：“I think I'll be able to take care of Mrs. Hayes on my own from hereon, Jason.”；信里：“Jason, Willoughby here.”，还叫他 Dipshit）、
  警佐 Cedric Connolly（电话里：“…as things stand, Jason.”）、他妈（“Jason!”）、片尾 Abercrombie（“You did good, Jason. You did real good.”）。
- 他叫警佐 Sarge（电话：“Hey, Sarge.”）。他叫 Red “Red”“boy”，叫 James “the town midget”。
- **Momma Dixon**（Sandy Martin 演）：抽烟、偏见重；给他出主意 “Why don't you fuck her friends over?”；
  “Off to see your fancy woman?” “I don't got a fancy woman.” “Yeah. I know!”
- 他爸已死；信里：“since your dad died and you had to go look after your Mom”。
- 他接家里电话：“The Dixon residence.”
- 警校：“You've been there three years. Not counting the five years at the Academy.” “Six! If you count the year you were held back.”（谁说哪半句不完全确定，大意：警局三年，警校五年，算上留级六年。）
- 他自己说：“I didn't used to be very good at English at school… Cos you need English, really, if you wanna be a cop.”
  “Well, all you can do is try, as my Momma says.”

## 场次（与他有关的，按片中顺序）

1. 广告牌刚贴上：他冲 Red 嚷，“I could arrest you right now… for emptying out your bucket.”
2. 打电话把 Willoughby 从复活节晚饭上叫起来。
3. 局里：Cedric 跟 Red 说 legal “ramifications”；Dixon 拿 Mildred 的逻辑噎 Cedric：“I didn't call you an idiot. I asked if you was an idiot. It was a question.”
   Willoughby 护他：“He's a good man. At heart.” 别人：“He tortured a guy in custody, Bill.” “There was no… real evidence.”
4. Main Street：揪 Red，“You think I wouldn't take you out, right here on Main Street, Red?” Willoughby：“Are you drunk right now?”
5. 酒吧台球桌：跟 Red 抬杠（Cuba / Wyoming）；被告知 Willoughby 得了胰腺癌（“Oh. You didn't know.”）；Mildred：“Ain't it about time you got home to your momma, Dixon?” 他：“I told her I was gonna be out 'til twelve. Actually.”
6. 局里：Willoughby 吼他 “Stop reading fucking comics”。Mildred 来时他说 “persons of color-torturing business”，“My momma watches that station!”
7. 家里看电视：Donald Sutherland（《Don't Look Now》）；他妈出主意整 Mildred 的朋友。
8. 抓 Denise：“Two marijuana cigarettes. Big ones.” 请法官不准保释。Mildred：“Your momma been coaching ya?” “No. My momma doesn't do that.”
9. Willoughby 自杀那天早上：他戴耳机跟着 ABBA《Chiquitita》哼，全局在看新闻（soundtrakd 已核）。
   之后他走过街、上二楼、把 Red 扔出窗外（一个长镜头；配乐 Monsters of Folk《His Master's Voice》）。“See, Red? I got issues with white folks too…”
10. Abercrombie 到任：“What happened to your hands?” “…throwing some guy out of a fucking window. Y'know, the usual.” 交枪和警徽：“I can't find my badge… Maybe I lost it or dropped it when I was doing the window guy?” 被开除。
11. 家里：他妈要去局里替他求情、要钱；“I don't know what the compensation scheme is for when you throw a guy out of a window, Mom.”
12. 警佐来电：Anne 送来了 Willoughby 给他的信；等人走光再来拿，拿完把钥匙留下。
13. 信全文要点：makings of a really good cop / deep down a decent man / “I do, Dipshit.” / too angry since dad died / 想当 Detective / 需要的是 Love → Calm → Thought / “You don't even need a gun.” / “Hate never solved nothing.” / “No-one'll think you're gay. And if they do, arrest 'em for homophobia!” / “things are gonna change for you… I can feel it.”
14. 燃烧瓶：他抱 Angela 的案卷跳窗，James 用外套扑火。（配乐《Last Rose of Summer》，片头也用过。）
15. 医院与 Red 同房：Red 要给他倒橙汁、找吸管；“I'm sorry, Welby.” “…For throwing you out the window.” Red：“And stop fucking crying. The salt'll just fuck up your wounds.”
16. 酒吧：听见 Idaho 车牌那人吹嘘；那人叫他 “Burnt-face-Jake”；他抓花那人的脸、挨揍：“I lost my badge. I can't remember where I lost it.” “I know I started it. I scratched you up like a bitch.”
17. 回家锁在浴室：“Just leave me, don't look at me, Momma.” … “I'm alright, Momma. It's all gonna be alright.”
18. 告诉 Mildred：“I didn't want you to give up hope, y'know?”
19. Abercrombie：DNA 不符，那人在国外服役（“It was sandy.”）。“I found my badge after all.”
20. 片尾：电话里提议去 Idaho；车上 Mildred 承认放火，“Well, who the Hell else would it have been?” “You sure about this?” “Not really. You?” “Not really.” “I guess we can decide along the way.”
